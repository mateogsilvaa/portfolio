What used to be scattered across result sheets and each season's websites, now in one place that updates itself.

01Rankings that compare everything

By event, year and age group, plus a points ranking to compare marks from different events on the same scale.

02Every athlete's career

A profile with their whole history and year-by-year progression, and head-to-head comparisons.

03Who's going to Worlds?

Entry standards for the World Championships, Europeans and Olympics, next to each athlete's marks.

04Reasons to come back

Records of the week, a new game every day and live results.

03 · Creator2026

Bicifastness.

A league for people who ride BiciMAD: your daily commute earns points. Madrid is the circuit.

Visit the site ↗

No need to change your route or ride faster: your usual BiciMAD ride already counts.

01Upload your ride

You ride, upload the screenshot and earn points for distance, pace and consistency.

02Compete with your peers

Groups of about 30 riders at your level, reshuffled every Monday.

03Conquer Madrid

Clans split the stations on the map. If a clan stops riding, it loses them.

04 · Creator2026

Tripfinder.

Cheap flights for getaways from Madrid that cost less than usual.

Visit the site ↗

A €40 flight might be a bargain or just the usual price. Tripfinder tells you which.

01Only what drops in price

It checks prices every 12 hours across all airlines and only shows what's below normal.

02Price alerts

Follow a flight, set a limit and get an email when it drops below it.

03Made for group trips

Share the trip, the group votes on the options and you add it to your calendar.
